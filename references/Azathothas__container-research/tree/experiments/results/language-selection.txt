== reading
  Static, PT_INTERP-free artefact: all three.
  Single-threaded at main(): rust and c. Go starts its scheduler first,
    which is why a Go process cannot unshare(CLONE_NEWUSER) even
    unconfined (paper §3.5 F11) and why PR_SET_PDEATHSIG is unreliable
    in a Go supervisor (§10.6).
  LD_PRELOAD interposer: read the table above rather than this line —
    a Go c-shared object *does* hook the symbol, and the cost shows up
    in the host-threads and object-size columns, not in a yes/no.
  Independent of all of it, paper §9.3: no preload of any language sees
    a Go *payload*'s lchown, cgo or not. The interpose tier's ceiling is
    a property of the payload, never of the interposer.
  TOOL.md §3 carries the decision these rows support.
