== reading
  The surviving half of this tier is the preload, not the tracer: the
  runtime's filter denies ptrace(2) and process_vm_readv(2), which is
  both of the tracer's channels (paper §3.7a).
  Path mapping and ownership faking are different jobs. An interposer
  that maps paths still passes the real uid/gid to the kernel, so §9.1
  stands until the same library also answers chown — which is the
  fakeroot half of TOOL.md's interposer spec, not the fakechroot half.
