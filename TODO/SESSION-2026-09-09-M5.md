## The four findings worth carrying forward

1. ⛔ **The banner was lying.** `mode=` printed the rung the PROBE selects and
   `podbox_enter` chroots on every machine, so this host announced
   `namespaces: as configured; mounts: full` for a payload with neither.
2. ⛔ **docker's exit-code discriminator is the flag parser**, not the kind of
   mistake: 125 for anything the parser refuses, 1 for anything a verb refuses
   after that. Four of eight cases were wrong, and 126 was folded into 127.
3. ⛔ **A ten-row matrix found five defects a one-image test would not**, each
   passing on some rows: an image with no `PATH`, `./` as a tar member, a
   symlinked `/etc/ssl/certs`, a symlinked `/lib`, and a CAfile at a path the
   image's TLS stack does not read.
4. ⛔ **The deep review found what the commit message had already claimed was
   fixed**: a fourth declaration of `EXIT_RUNTIME_ERROR`, in a crate the
   consolidation missed. Check 20 holds it now.
